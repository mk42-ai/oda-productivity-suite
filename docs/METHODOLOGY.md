### Provenance Tiers & Evidence Architecture
To preserve analytical rigor and transparency, all statements within the reference dossier are stratified across five defined provenance tiers:

- **Verified**:
  Directly corroborated by primary official records, government releases (e.g., WAM article 2026-06-23 naming H.E. Dr Thani bin Ahmed Al Zeyoudi, Minister of Foreign Trade, as Chairman; WAM NextGenFDI anchor February 2024), verified LinkedIn corporate entities, and verified third-party corporate statements (Qualcomm, Tenstorrent, TechAfrica News).

- **Company-Reported**:
  Operational metrics disclosed directly by AIREV and OnDemand leadership or product collateral (e.g., 4M+ platform users, 300+ autonomous agents, 50+ languages, 6.8T tokens processed in Q2 2026, and 25,000 users within the first six weeks post-launch with Core42 in July 2024). These metrics are explicitly labelled as "company-reported" and are not represented as independently audited financial statements.

- **Reported / Unconfirmed**:
  Secondary market reporting regarding capital rounds and corporate valuation (e.g., reported ~US$200M valuation and ongoing Series A2 funding rounds). Exact valuations and cap-table breakdowns are flagged as "not publicly disclosed" and status retained as "reported, unconfirmed".

- **Internal Documents (Unverified Publicly)**:
  Materials located in internal archives or confidential media rooms (including the AIREV Investment Memorandum, redacted Series A2 term sheets, a US growth investor term sheet, chairman strategic overviews, and ADX listing landscape studies). To protect institutional confidentiality, these records are cited solely to establish document existence; all monetary figures, valuations, and commercial terms are strictly withheld, and confidence is marked low.

- **Dead Link → Archive Fallback**:
  Original publisher URLs that have degraded (returning HTTP 404), such as Zawya press releases for VentureWave Capital (April 2025) and Supermicro certification (October 2025), Middle East AI News regarding the GITEX 2025 Intel MoU, and GEC Newswire regarding Intel Core Ultra on-device agents. Each is catalogued with a verified Wayback Machine archive snapshot.
